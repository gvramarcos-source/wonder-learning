What does Wonder Learning offer companies?

For companies with a small cohort of new managers, Wonder Learning runs a series of short, small-group workshops on core management topics such as giving feedback, delegation, and 1:1s. Managers learn each skill together, practice it with peers, and use it at work between workshops.

The gap

Too small for a big program. Too important to leave to chance.

A few promotions in one quarter. A growing department. A new layer of team leads. It's rarely enough people to justify a full leadership program, but every one of them needs the same foundations, and they need them now.

Left alone, each new manager works it out separately, and your teams feel the difference. For managers who supervise one or two people, 59% have had no management training at all. Source: 2018 West Monroe Partners study, reported by HR Dive.

The approach

Small group training, built to stick

Small on purpose

A small group means everyone speaks, practices, and gets answers to their own questions. Nobody sits at the back.

Short and focused

Each workshop takes on one topic and goes straight to what managers can use, without pulling them away from their teams for days.

A series, not a one-off

Between workshops, managers try each skill at work, then bring back what happened. That's where learning turns into habit.

Core management topics

We choose the topics your new managers need most and put them in the order that makes sense for your group.

  • The IC-to-manager mindset shift
  • 1:1 best practices
  • Giving feedback
  • Delegation
  • Coaching skills
  • Team culture
  • Prioritization
  • Stakeholder management
  • Change management

What happens in a workshop

Learn the essentials

The ideas that matter on this topic, and the mistakes new managers commonly make.

Practice with peers

Work through realistic scenarios together. Managers try the skill in a safe space before using it with their teams.

Leave with a plan

Open Q&A, then a personal action plan to put the skill to use back at work.
