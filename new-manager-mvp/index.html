What is New Manager MVP?

New Manager MVP is a 1:1 training and coaching program for first-time managers: five personalized sessions, delivered virtually over 30 to 45 days, for $999. Each session focuses on a skill you need most right now, such as giving feedback, delegating, or making the shift from individual contributor to manager, followed by coaching to help you put it into practice.

Who it's for

The Newly Promoted Manager

You were just promoted and you're already feeling the gap between what you knew how to do and what you're now expected to lead. You want real training, tailored to your situation, that you can start now.

The HR or L&D Buyer

Someone on your team was just promoted and needs development fast. You don't want to wait months for a cohort to form. New Manager MVP deploys immediately, adapts to your company context, and gives you something concrete to report on.

What's included

Five focused sessions, sequenced to build on each other.

  1. Session 1

    Orientation and Priority Setting

    We assess where you are, what your team needs, and identify the two or three skills that will have the most immediate impact. The program is built from there — not from a predetermined script.

  2. Session 2

    Core Skill — Personalized to You

    One of your highest-priority skills in depth. Typically covers communication, feedback, or the IC-to-manager mindset shift — depending on what matters most in your context.

  3. Session 3

    Core Skill — Personalized to You

    A second high-priority skill, with time to reflect on and integrate what you've practiced from Session 2.

  4. Session 4

    Core Skill — Personalized to You

    Third skill focus, plus a check-in on how earlier skill-building is showing up in your day-to-day work.

  5. Session 5

    Integration and Next Steps

    A review of what's changed, what's still developing, and a clear picture of where to focus after the program ends.

All sessions are delivered virtually via video call. Sessions are typically 60 minutes each. You'll complete a short intake form before Session 1, and you'll get a check-in between each session.

Worth knowing before you book

New Manager MVP is not an executive leadership program — it's built for first-time managers, not directors or VPs. It's not self-paced. The value comes from live, 1:1 interaction; recorded modules are not part of this program. It's not a certification. And it's not a long-term retainer — five sessions is the defined scope.

If any of those are dealbreakers, it's better to know now. If they're not — book a consultation.
