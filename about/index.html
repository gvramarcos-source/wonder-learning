Why I built Wonder Learning

The story that almost every new manager tells is the same: promoted, congratulated, then left on their own to figure it out. No training. No clear expectations. Just the assumption that being good at the job automatically translates into being good at leading others.

It doesn't. And the research backs that up: a 2018 West Monroe Partners study found that 59% of managers who supervise one or two people have had no management training at all.

I've seen what good manager development looks like, and I've seen what happens when it's skipped. Most of what's available in the market doesn't serve the person who just got promoted last month. The executive programs are too long and too expensive. The self-serve courses don't adapt to your situation and don't hold you accountable.

Wonder Learning and Coaching exists to fix that — with structured, 1:1 coaching and training that meets you where you are and builds the skills that actually matter in your first year as a manager.

Our mission

We believe that the wonder of learning is worth keeping alive – even if it's for work.

We do that by empowering new managers to succeed through personalized coaching and training, fostering a growth mindset, and igniting the wonder of learning in the workplace.

The facilitator-coach model

Traditional development programs are stuck in multi-hour learning sessions that mimic an in-person classroom in a virtual world. You get something different here: focused 1:1 learning sessions on the skills you choose, followed by coaching that helps you put them into practice.

That closes the gap between learning a skill and using it, as quickly as possible, when it matters most.
